## Falsification

- **`target="_blank"` fails 3.2.1 because a new window is a change of context:** the window opens on link **activation**, not on receiving focus. Understanding example of failure is launching a window **when a component receives focus**.
- **Cookie Preferences / first-layer modal steals focus, so 3.2.1 fails:** first layer is auto-shown on load (`autoShow`), not on focus of a host control. Preferences modal opens on **click**. Moving focus into a dialog after activation is expected, not an on-focus trigger.
- **Clicking theme-toggle both focuses and changes `data-theme`:** Understanding explicitly allows click-to-activate on buttons. Keyboard Tab to the toggle does not call `toggleTheme`.
- **FAQ `<details>` opening is a change of content:** G107/Understanding: expanding outlines are not a change of context unless they also move focus or change the page. Native summary does not open on focus alone.
- **Issue search** `3.2.1 OR "on focus" OR autofocus OR "change of context"`: no matching open or closed issue for this SC. No issue created (report-only).
